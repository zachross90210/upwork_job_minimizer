# Privacy Policy — Upwork Job Tile Minimizer

Last updated: 2026-11-09

## What this extension does

Upwork Job Tile Minimizer lets you hide job tiles on the Upwork Find Work feed and remembers which tiles you hid.

## Data collected

The extension stores only:

- Upwork job opening IDs (UIDs) for tiles you chose to hide

This data is saved with Chrome’s `storage.sync` API so your hidden tiles can sync across browsers where you are signed into the same Chrome profile.

## Data not collected

The extension does **not** collect, transmit, or sell:

- Your name, email, or Upwork account details
- Job descriptions, proposals, or message contents
- Browsing history outside the matched Upwork Find Work pages
- Analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking cookies

## Network access

The extension runs only as a content script on `https://www.upwork.com/nx/find-work/*`. It does not send your data to any developer server.

## Third parties

No third-party services, analytics, or advertising SDKs are included.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project’s GitHub repository.
